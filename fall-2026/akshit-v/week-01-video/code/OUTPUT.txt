the loss floor
------------------------------------------------------------------
   loss at step 600        = 0.6131
   entropy of the corpus   = 0.6109
   the objective bottoms out AT the corpus's own entropy, not at 0.
   a perfect fit to a contradictory corpus is still a perfect fit.

   full trajectory (601 steps) -> code/trajectory.json

The loop minimized its loss by matching the corpus, and the corpus
was wrong. Truth never entered the objective.
