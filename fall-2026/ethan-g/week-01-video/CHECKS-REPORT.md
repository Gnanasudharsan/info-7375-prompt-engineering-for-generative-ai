## Teaching arc detail
- **FRAMEWORK before examples** — B02 establishes which figures are published and
  checkable before any derivation runs.
- **WORKED EXAMPLE** — B03 works the reading comparison end to end; B06 works the
  compute comparison end to end. Two instances, same method.
- **FALSIFIABILITY** — B04 is the falsifying move: the claim "this is a
  measurement" is tested by changing an input and showing the answer move 1,141
  years. B07 applies the same test across two circulating claims.
- **SCAFFOLDED TASK** — B11's prompt is read verbatim, discussed, and given a
  concrete pass/fail (a 2× swing means you found the hidden parameter).
- **BOOKENDS** — three: B00 (cold open), B01 (BLUF), B11 (handoff, which is also the last slide). There is no separate title outro. The boundary statement (B10) sits between the verdict and the handoff.
- **NO-SOURCE-NO-VERDICT** — B09 judges only the chapter's own method, with the
  source and citations recorded in SOURCES.md / FACTCHECK.md.

## Steelman check
B05 exists specifically so the reel does not overclaim. The falsifying beat (B04)
could be misread as "the comparison is bogus"; B05 states the opposite — the
claim that survives every rate is true and worth making. The verdict in B09 rests
on that distinction rather than on debunking.
