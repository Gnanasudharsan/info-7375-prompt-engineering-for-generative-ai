## B11 — the handoff prompt (HANDOFF LAW)
> Take an impressive number from my own field and restate it as the division it actually is. List every input, mark which ones are measured and which are assumed, then show me how far the answer moves across a reasonable range for each assumption.

This is the last slide of the video. It shows the prompt only, with no Claude
output — it is for the viewer to run. Read aloud verbatim, then discussed: the final clause is what makes it worth
running — it replaces "is this number right?" (unanswerable from outside) with
"how far can it move?" (answerable by anyone with the inputs). The stated test —
a swing of 2× means you have found the hidden parameter — gives the viewer a
pass/fail they can apply without the chapter. It extends the episode into the
viewer's own field rather than asking them to re-read this one.
