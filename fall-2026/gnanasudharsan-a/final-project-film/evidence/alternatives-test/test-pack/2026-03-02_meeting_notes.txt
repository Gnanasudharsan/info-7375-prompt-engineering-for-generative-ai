Context
The new Shipment Tracking Service stores shipment records and their scan
events (pickup, hub arrival, out for delivery, delivered). We need to choose
its primary datastore before Sprint 6 starts.

Options discussed
1. PostgreSQL (managed, single primary + read replica)
2. DynamoDB
3. Keep writing into the legacy order database

Decision
We will use PostgreSQL for the tracking service.

Reasons
- Shipments, scan events, and customer accounts are strongly relational, and
  support staff need ad-hoc joins across them.
- The team already runs PostgreSQL for billing, so on-call knowledge and
  backup tooling carry over.
- Current volume (about 40,000 scan events per day) fits comfortably on one
  primary with a read replica.
- Elena needs SQL access for the weekly delivery-time report.

Rejected
- DynamoDB: access patterns are still changing, and designing the keys up
  front felt premature. Revisit if volume grows sharply.
- Legacy order DB: schema is overloaded and owned by another team.
