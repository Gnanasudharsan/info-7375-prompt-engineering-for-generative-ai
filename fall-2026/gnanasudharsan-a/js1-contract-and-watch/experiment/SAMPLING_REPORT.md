## 3. Verdict Spread & Repeatability
* **Unanimous Postings (4/5):** For 4 out of 5 postings, Claude returned the exact same verdict across all 4 independent runs. Verdicts were stable across these 4 runs under identical prompt and system conditions.
* **The Split Posting (`4613568008`):** Posting `4613568008` (Research Engineer, Machine Learning / RL) exhibited variance, splitting **3–1** across the 4 primary sampling runs (`NOT_RELEVANT`, `RELEVANT`, `RELEVANT`, `RELEVANT`). Factoring in the preliminary smoke test (`NOT_RELEVANT`), its aggregate distribution across 5 total executions is **3 `RELEVANT` vs 2 `NOT_RELEVANT`**, showing that a single prompt does not guarantee absolute deterministic output on borderline technical postings.

## 4. Analytical Conclusions: Where Claude and Human Judgment Diverge
* **Absolute Agreement on Clear-Cut Postings:** Claude perfectly matched my hand labels on the straightforward `NOT_RELEVANT` cases (`4980430008` due to its 5–10+ years experience requirement, and `5264619008` due to its London, UK location).
* **Systematic Disagreements on Research-Blended Roles:** For the two Research Engineer / Scientist postings (`5438044008` RL Frontiers and `4631822008` Alignment), Claude unanimously judged them `NOT_RELEVANT` (4/4 times), whereas I labeled them `RELEVANT`. 
* **Underlying Reasoning Analysis:** Reviewing the full saved reasoning in `experiment/runs/sampling_20261029T0445Z/`, Claude's actual text reveals distinct reasons for each disagreement:
  * For `5438044008` (RL Frontiers, logged in `5438044008_r01.json`), Claude agreed there was “no explicit year count” and no PhD required, but judged the role `NOT_RELEVANT` because the qualifications “clearly target senior frontier-scale practitioners” and noted that “The $500,000-$850,000 salary band confirms a senior or staff level.”
  * For `4631822008` (Alignment, logged in `4631822008_r01.json`), Claude disagreed on role type, stating that “The representative projects are mostly research experiments… Building jailbreak-evaluation tooling is the only clearly engineering-focused item.”
* **Meaning of the Divergence:** These disagreements trace directly to two nameable gaps between my labeling rule and Claude's evaluation: (1) my rule only rejects explicit years, senior titles, or a PhD, whereas Claude reads qualifications and pay bands as implicit level signals; and (2) my rule counts designing and running experiments as hands-on evaluation/engineering, whereas Claude counts them as pure research. Because my prompt (`prompts/judge_baseline.md`) never stated my "experiments-as-evaluate" interpretation, the model applied its own standard.

## 5. Limitations & Caveats
* **Repeatability is Not Correctness:** As illustrated by postings `5438044008` and `4631822008`, Claude can be 100% repeatable (4/4 unanimous) while consistently disagreeing with human ground-truth labels. 
* **Small Sample Size:** Evaluating only 5 dev postings provides directional insights but is insufficient to make sweeping statistical claims about model accuracy.
- **Interpreting Disagreements:** The disagreements are consistent (not random) and all point one way: Claude is stricter on research-blended roles. That pattern is consistent with a gap in my labeling rule and prompt (my narrow reading of "research" and my explicit-only level test), but it is equally consistent with Claude applying a reasonable standard I didn't write down. Five postings can't tell these apart.
* **Backend Divergence:** Because these runs executed via `JOBWATCH_BACKEND=claude_cli`, minor formatting or contextual wrappers injected by the CLI environment represent a distinct operational condition compared to direct API calls.
