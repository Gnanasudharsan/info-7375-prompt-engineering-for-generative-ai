# PROMPTS

No generation prompts. Every picture is drawn in code: the 15 body scenes are HyperFrames compositions (HTML + GSAP, `code/hf_scenes.py`), the opening and closing are deterministic Remotion scenes, and captions are a HyperFrames edit pass (`code/hf_edit.py`). No image or video generation, no paid calls. (v1 used Manim drawings; see `_v1/`.)
