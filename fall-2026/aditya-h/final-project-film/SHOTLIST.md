# SHOTLIST: ProductLens AI pitch film (v2)

Show-tell: one drawing per beat, 1–3 word labels, and narration that carries the idea. One running example (a PM at an
online shoe store weighing a one-page checkout) goes through the whole film.
**Subheaders** (bottom centre, every body beat): say what that part of the film is about. On every drawing of the unbuilt
product the subheader ends "· concept sketch" (the assignment's "mock-ups labelled constructed on screen"). B05/B06 show
crops of Aditya's real ChatGPT run; their subheaders end "· Oct 25 · colours inverted". The crops are made by
`prepare_crops.py`: cut from his screenshots, dark mode inverted to light so the text passes GATE T contrast, and enlarged.
Nothing is retouched.
No ShowTellCard beats; no terms card (the script explains each term as it's first used).
