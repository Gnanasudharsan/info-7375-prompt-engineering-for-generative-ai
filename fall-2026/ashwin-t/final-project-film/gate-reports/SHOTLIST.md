# Shot list — TraceCase pitch film

Every body beat is one drawn isometric Manim scene (show-tell). Bookends: hesitant writer and title outro.
Cast kept for the whole film: the service (server stack; lights turn terracotta when it breaks), log pages,
the trace (span bars), the agent (magnifier), the proposal card, the gate (barrier arm) and the approval seal.
Draft 2: every body beat carries a small heading naming which of the seven questions it answers; flow arrows
connect cause to effect (B00, B02, B05, B08, B10, B12); the B06 loop has direction arrows; a proper mouse
pointer replaces the kit cursor.
Draft 3: each beat's main picture is on screen from its first frame.
