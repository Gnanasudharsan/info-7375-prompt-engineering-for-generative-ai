# PROMPTS — Marino Desk Assistant: A Pitch

No generation prompts. Nothing in this film was made by an image, video or voice-cloning model:

- Every picture is drawn in code (`scenes.py`, Manim), using the toolkit's show-tell `iso_kit.py` primitives
  plus my own helpers (desk bell, browser window, answer bubbles, contract slip, crates).
- The narration is the script in `beat_sheet.json` (Claude drafted it from my decisions; see SOURCES.md), read by
  the toolkit's local Kokoro TTS voice `am_michael`. It is free and offline, with no account and no key.
- No slots were left open for generated media, so there are no beat-prefixed prompts to list.

The Claude Code prompts that drove the build are in `BUILD-PROMPT.md`.
