# Shot list: Electricity Supplier Check (pitch film)

The format is show-tell, the assignment's pitch format: one simple drawn picture per beat, at most three short labels, and the narration carries the explanation.
- **Body beats:** native Manim vector drawings in this folder's `scenes.py`, using the show-tell iso kit pasted in.
- **Visual cast changed 2026-10-14:** the show-tell kit's cardboard boxes were replaced with electricity objects and plain panels, at the narrator's request.
- **B00:** a Manim title card (`B00_Title`). It replaced the library `FormACard` on 2026-10-14 so the title could be larger.
- **Media:** no stock, no screenshots and no generated media.
