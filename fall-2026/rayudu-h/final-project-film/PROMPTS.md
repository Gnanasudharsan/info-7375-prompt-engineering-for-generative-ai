# Prompts

**No generation prompts.** The film uses no generated images, video or music.
- Every picture, including the title card, is native vector code (Manim, in `scenes.py`).
- Narration is free, local Kokoro TTS (`am_onyx`).

Claude (Claude Code) drafted the narration and wrote the build. The prompts and commands that rebuild the film are in `BUILD-PROMPT.md`. What Claude contributed is listed in `SOURCES.md`.
