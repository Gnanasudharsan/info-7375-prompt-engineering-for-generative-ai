# Netflix Timed Text Style Guide: reading speed, line length, timing

- Fetched: 2026-10-14 (Claude Code, WebFetch)

## English (USA) Timed Text Style Guide
URL: https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide
- "Adult programs: Up to 20 characters per second"
- "Children's programs: Up to 17 characters per second"
- "42 characters per line"
- The fetched page does not say whether spaces or punctuation count toward reading speed.

## Subtitle Timing Guidelines
URL: https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394
- "Subtitles should not be any shorter in duration than 20 frames (or 4/5 sec)."
- "Subtitles must have a minimum of 2 frames between them."
- "Gaps between subtitles should either be 2 frames or half a second or more."
- Shot changes: "within half a second past the shot change"; "you should still set the out-time to two frames before the shot change."; "the out-time may be set to 12 frames after the shot change"
- No characters-per-second figure on this page.

## General Requirements
URL: https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617
- "Minimum duration: 5/6 (five-sixths) of a second per subtitle event (e.g. 20 frames for 24fps)"
- "Maximum duration: 7 seconds per subtitle event"
- "2 lines maximum."

## Subtitle Templates
URL: https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/219375728
- "Avoid condensing text unnecessarily and do not paraphrase."
- Template reading speed: "Adult programs: Up to 17 characters per second"; "Children's programs: Up to 15 characters per second". These are template limits and differ from the English TTSG's 20 and 17.
- The page does not mention QC, rejection or flagged files.
