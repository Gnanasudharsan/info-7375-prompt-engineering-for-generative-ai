## Reading speed parameters (Webhelp)
- Fetched: 2026-10-14 (Claude Code, WebFetch)
- URL: https://eztitles.com/Webhelp/EZTitles/project_settings_reading_speed.htm
- Limits can be "expressed as exact CPS or WPM values".
- Counting option: "Adds white space characters (regular space, tab, ideographic space, etc)" to the calculation.
- Punctuation: "It is turned on by default and will count any punctuation marks towards the reading speed limits."
- Flagging: "Subtitles identified as Below reading speed indicate" the viewer may struggle to finish reading; "those identified as Above reading speed indicate that they stay on screen presumably longer than required."
- "the Check Subtitles feature define the margins for subtitles' calculated reading speed."

Note: EZTitles' "Below reading speed" label means too fast to read, which is the opposite of the everyday sense.
