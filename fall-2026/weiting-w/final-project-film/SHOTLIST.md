## Standing constraints every scene is built to

- One drawing per beat; the narration carries the idea. Labels are one to three words, placed
  beside their object, never inside an outline and never in terracotta.
- Terracotta appears only on dots, one curve, a scan line and a check — never on type.
- Each scene constructs and lands its labels before the clip midpoint, holds through it with
  nothing in flight, and adds one new non-text shape afterwards. GATE T and Gate V both sample
  the midpoint, and Gate A requires a membership change.
- Continuity: the project slab, its pages and blocks, the string, the two gates, the lamp and
  the checklist slip persist across the beats that share them.
- Every beat that describes work not yet done carries the word "proposed" on screen.
