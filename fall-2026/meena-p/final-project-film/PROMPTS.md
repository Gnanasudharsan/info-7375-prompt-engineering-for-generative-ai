# PROMPTS — Cite & Seek pitch film

No generation prompts. Nothing in this film was made by an image, video or music generation model.

- Drawings: written as code in scenes.py (Manim) and rendered locally.
- Bookends (BIDEA, BDEFS, BHTF): the toolkit's own Remotion components, with the props in beat_sheet.json.
- Narration: local Kokoro text-to-speech, voice am_michael, reading the script in beat_sheet.json.

Open slots needing a generated asset: none.
